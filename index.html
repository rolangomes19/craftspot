About Us

Aquiline International is an aviation solutions provider founded in 1999. Based in the United Arab Emirates and Moldova, we own and operate a fleet of Boeing 747, 777 and 737 aircraft.

We move cargo and passengers on our own aircraft, and we provide charter, ACMI and aircraft leasing for customers who need capacity on their terms.

From scheduled movements to on-demand charters, we put aircraft, crew and operations behind the mission. Find out more about us and our history.
